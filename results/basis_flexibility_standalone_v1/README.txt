PAIRED BASIS-FLEXIBILITY VALIDATION

Fixed ARMA(2,1) estimator; 3, 5 and 7 lag coefficients.
200 replications for each error-process and curve scenario.
Identical simulated data used across basis dimensions.

Coverage targets the original generating curve, not its projection into a restricted basis. Restricted-basis coverage therefore includes approximation error.

Paired contrasts use replications where all three fits succeeded. Failures are recorded separately.
MC intervals quantify simulation uncertainty, not empirical country-coefficient uncertainty.

These complete-data experiments do not establish performance under informative missingness, changing coefficients or all possible exposure and error processes.
The model-selection procedure is not repeated.
